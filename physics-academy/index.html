🎢 Newton's Playground

Laws of Motion, Work, Energy, and Conservation — the foundation of mechanics.

Lecture 1: Newton's Laws — Getting Things Moving Live

Learn how to solve problems involving forces, friction, and acceleration on a horizontal surface. Includes an interactive Python calculator!

Lecture 2: Work and Energy — The Transfer of Motion Coming Soon

Master the Work-Energy Theorem and learn how energy transforms between kinetic, potential, and thermal forms.

Lecture 3: Power — The Rate of Doing Work Coming Soon

Understand the relationship between power, force, and velocity in practical contexts.

Lecture 4: Conservation of Linear Momentum — The Heart of Collisions Coming Soon

Lecture 5: Impulse — Force Over Time Coming Soon

Connect force, time, and momentum change in this deep dive into impulse and its applications.

📚 About This Series

This Physics Academy is designed specifically for 100-level Nigerian university students tackling PHY 105 (Mechanics and Properties of Matter). Each lesson uses past questions from institutions like UNILAG, UI, UNIBEN, and OAU to teach fundamental physics principles through active problem-solving. Where necessary, we also reinforce the underlying mathematics.

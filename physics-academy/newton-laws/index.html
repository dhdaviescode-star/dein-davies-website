⚛️ Lecture 1: Newton's Laws & Kinematics

Mastering classical mechanics, friction, and dynamic relative motion.

📝 Problem 1: Block on a Rough Horizontal Surface

"A 5 kg object is placed on a rough horizontal surface. A horizontal force of 30 N is applied. If the coefficient of kinetic friction is 0.4, calculate the acceleration of the object."
  1. Vertical equilibrium: $N = mg = 5 \text{ kg} \times 9.81 \text{ m/s}^2 = 49.05 \text{ N}$
  2. Kinetic friction force: $f_k = \mu_k \cdot N = 0.4 \times 49.05 \text{ N} = 19.62 \text{ N}$
  3. Apply Newton's 2nd Law horizontally: $F_{\text{net}} = F_{\text{app}} - f_k = 30 \text{ N} - 19.62 \text{ N} = 10.38 \text{ N}$
  4. Solve for acceleration: $a = \frac{F_{\text{net}}}{m} = \frac{10.38}{5} = 2.08 \text{ m/s}^2$
✅ Final Answer: 2.08 m/s²

📝 Problem 2: High-Speed Train & Inspection Car

"A high-speed train traveling along a straight track at a constant speed of 30 m/s passes a stationary inspection car at $t = 0\text{ s}$. The inspection car accelerates uniformly from rest at $2.0\text{ m/s}^2$ up to its top speed of $50\text{ m/s}$, continuing at constant speed thereafter. When and where does it catch the train?"
  1. Acceleration Phase ($t_1$): $t_1 = \frac{v_{\text{max}}}{a} = \frac{50}{2.0} = 25\text{ s}$. Car covers $x_{2,1} = \frac{1}{2}(2.0)(25)^2 = 625\text{ m}$.
  2. Train position at $t_1$: $x_{1,1} = (30\text{ m/s})(25\text{ s}) = 750\text{ m}$. Gap remaining: $\Delta x = 750 - 625 = 125\text{ m}$.
  3. Phase 2 Relative Velocity: $v_{\text{rel}} = 50 - 30 = 20\text{ m/s}$. Gap closed in $\Delta t = \frac{125}{20} = 6.25\text{ s}$.
  4. Total time & distance: $t_{\text{total}} = 25 + 6.25 = 31.25\text{ s}$; $x_{\text{total}} = 30 \times 31.25 = 937.5\text{ m}$.
✅ Catch-Up Point: t = 31.25 s, x = 937.5 m
